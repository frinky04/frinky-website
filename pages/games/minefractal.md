Explore a procedural voxel fractal-inspired world to find artifacts and portals.

* 25,000+ block render distance.
* Traversal tools: Grapple hook, wings, pickaxe, * block, locator.
* Infinite procedural world (X, Y and Z)
* Procedurally generated ambient music.
* Many fractals to explore.
* Find artefacts using the locator to unlock various * things.
* Markov chain text generation.
* Speak with artefacts and observe their behavior.
* Full controller support
* Windows + Linux.
